LIVES

Everyone starts with 3 lives. Each dart hit costs one. Lose all three and you drop to your knees: no moving, no shooting.

A teammate can revive you by holding E next to you. You come back with 1 life. Lose that one and you're out for the round: you'll watch your teammates' bodycams.

Nobody comes for you within the bleed-out timer? You're out.

MODES

Team Deathmatch: last team with someone standing wins. Time runs out? Most lives left wins.

Hunt: hiders get a prep phase to hide, build barricades and set traps while the hunters count with their eyes closed. Then the hunt starts. Hiders win if anyone survives or escapes once extraction opens.

JÜRGEN

In Jürgen Forest a neutral man with a katana guards his base. He attacks anyone who enters. Hit him enough and he'll sit down for a bit. Gunfire near his fence draws him out, and you can use that to lure enemies in.
